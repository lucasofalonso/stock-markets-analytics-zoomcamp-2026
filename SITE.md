Stock Market Analytics Zoomcamp is a completely free, hands-on course that takes you from financial data sources to a predictive model, a trading strategy, and an automated workflow that runs on its own. Five modules cover data sources, working with financial time series in pandas, analytical modeling, trading strategy simulation, and automation, followed by a final project. The course is run together with PythonInvest.

This course is educational. Nothing in it is investment advice, and there is no guarantee of profitability.
